# prime-radiant/tests/integration

Cross-module integration tests.

## Files

- `mod.rs` — module aggregator.
- `coherence_tests.rs` — full engine on realistic substrates.
- `gate_tests.rs` — 4-lane gate behaviour under different energy profiles.
- `governance_tests.rs` — policy bundle + witness + lineage flow.
- `graph_tests.rs` — substrate graph construction and traversal.
